🐺 ɫok̓ʷimasux̌ da ʔuligən 🌲

A ʔuligən runs to protect the last old-growth ʔaƛi — chase off the feller bunchers, gather native m̓ək̓ʷəǧi, and clean up the q̓əbac̓i left behind.

🎯 Goal

  • Destroy every feller buncher (the logging machines) in each level to restore it.
  • Collect native m̓ək̓ʷəǧi 🌱 and q̓əbac̓i 🗑️ along the way for points.
  • Avoid falling into ravines and rivers, and don't get hit by the machines — you only have 3 noqe (lives)!

⌨️ Keyboard Controls

  • ← → or A D — Run
  • Space or ↑ — Jump (press again in mid-air to double-jump!)
  • Shift — Dash (bursts of speed, smashes through log piles)
  • X — Howl & Bite attack (short range)
  • P or Esc — Pause

📱 Touch Controls

On phones and tablets, use the on-screen paw buttons: ◀▶ to run, ⤒ to jump, ⚡ to dash, and 🐾 to howl/bite.

🐾 Defeating Machines

Jump on top of a feller buncher to stomp it, or get close and use your Howl & Bite attack. Either way, it's replaced by a fresh little sapling!
